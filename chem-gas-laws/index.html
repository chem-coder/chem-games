Gas Laws

How a gas answers to temperature, pressure, and volume — one law at a time, each with its own lab.

Charles’s Law — volume follows temperature

At constant pressure, the volume of a gas is proportional to its temperature: warm a balloon and it swells, chill it and it shrinks. The particles inside don’t change — they just push harder or softer.

V1 / T1 = V2 / T2

Temperatures in kelvin — the proportionality only holds from absolute zero. (T in K = °C + 273.15)
