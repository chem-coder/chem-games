## What E1 feeds, by topic card

- **Measurement & Units (advanced):** compound + cubed-unit conversions (µg/L → oz/in³), sig figs through mixed operation chains, density-as-identification against a metals table (water displacement), all-three temperature scales, graph→equation reading. → Conveyor/Builder top rungs.
- **Matter:** particle-level diagram classification (element/compound/mixture), physical-vs-chemical separation.
- **Atomic Structure (advanced):** inverted isotope notation (mass 106, 58 n, 46 e⁻ → ¹⁰⁶Cd²⁺), electrons-vs-neutrons comparisons across five ions, reverse weighted-average abundance incl. non-natural enriched samples, atomic-history attribution grids (who measured what).
- **Periodic System:** family multi-selects incl. chalcogens/metalloids.
- **Formulas & Ions:** reverse criss-cross ("XCl₃, cation has 23 e⁻ → X = Fe").
- **Inorganic Nomenclature:** error-spotting on mixed name/formula pairings — a genuinely NEW rung above Name Builder's five.
- **Mole & Composition (advanced):** combustion analysis with O-by-difference, empirical→molecular with molar mass given as a *range*, percent-composition equivalence across CₙHₙ multiples.
- **Equations & Reaction Types (advanced):** five-element mineral balancing, sum-of-coefficients answer format, unbalanced equations embedded inside stoichiometry problems.
- **Stoichiometry (advanced):** numberless limiting-reagent reasoning (equal masses), answers reporting product **plus leftover excess**, limiting+yield stacked, solution-phase (volume × molarity both sides — the KCN/HCl 0.54 g HCN item).
- **Solutions:** dilution as a *procedure choice* (pick the pipetting recipe), ion-specific molarity (Na₂SO₄ → 0.752/0.376 M), mM↔M traps.

2. **Error-spotting nomenclature** — five name/formula pairs, tap the wrong ones; inverts Name Builder, generatable from the existing ion tables.
3. **Reverse-identity puzzles** — procedurally generatable, self-verifying; an infinite advanced rung for Atomic Structure.
4. **Identify-from-property-table** — density + displacement → which metal; clean lab-framed mini-game.
5. **Balancing answer-format variants** — sum-of-coefficients / one named coefficient / pick-the-balanced-equation: three scoring modes over the existing balancer engine.
6. **"What's present after the reaction?"** — end-state answers (product + leftovers), richer than a single number.
7. **Procedure selection** — dilution as apparatus-step choice.
