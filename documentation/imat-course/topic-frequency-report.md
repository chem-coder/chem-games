# IMAT Chemistry 2011–2024 — Topic Frequency Report

**Basis:** all 169 chemistry questions from the 14 official IMAT papers (2011–2024), extracted verbatim with answers matched from the official keys, tagged against an 18-topic taxonomy aligned to the official syllabus. Full data: [imat-chem-question-bank.json](imat-chem-question-bank.json).

**Section size:** 8–12 questions/year through 2021; **15 questions/year since the 2022 restructure.**

## Headline findings

1. **The Big Three carry 37% of the exam.** Chemical bonding (21), stoichiometry (21), and acids–bases (20) together account for 62 of 169 questions. Acids–bases is also *accelerating*: 8 of the last 45 questions (2022–24), the single biggest recent topic, and it's dominated by pH calculations — strong-acid/base pH, dilution effects, Kw reasoning, hydrolysis of salts.

2. **Redox is a metronome: exactly one question every single year.** And it's always the same *kind* of question — assign oxidation numbers, identify the oxidant/reductant, spot the redox reaction. In 14 years there is essentially **no galvanic/electrolytic-cell question** (cell potential never appears). The Oxidation-State Trainer is aimed at precisely what IMAT actually asks.

3. **Organic reactions have nearly vanished from the modern IMAT.** Five of the six organic-reaction questions are from 2011–2014; just one since (2022). Modern IMAT organic = **isomer counting/identification (steady ~1/year), functional-group recognition, and naming** — not reaction chemistry. (Reactions-lab remains valuable pedagogy, but for IMAT scoring, isomerism and functional groups are the priority.)

4. **Gas laws came back.** Zero questions 2011–2017 (except 2018's two), then 1 in 2023 and **3 in 2024** — partial pressures, P/T reasoning, unit conversions. Recent papers treat it as a full topic again.

5. **Atomic structure is quietly reliable** (1–2/year): electron configurations of *ions*, isoelectronic species, and proton/neutron/electron counting — highly gameable, typed-recall friendly.

6. **Near-zero topics:** kinetics (2 questions in 14 years), thermochemistry (0 tagged — energy appears only inside other questions), biochemistry (0 in the chemistry section — it lives in biology). Equilibrium and inorganic nomenclature are real but small (5 each).

## Question-style notes

- Five options A–E, single best answer, no calculator; every computation resolves to clean mental arithmetic (dilution ratios, integer mole ratios, pH of 10⁻ⁿ solutions).
- Recurring stoichiometry patterns: Avogadro-scale atom counting (3×), percentage yield (3×), formula from % composition, limiting reagent.
- Figure-dependent questions are rare (~1–2/year recently, mostly organic structures); nearly everything works as text.

## Data caveats

- 2021–2024 questions and keys were transcribed from image-based PDFs (AcadIMAT shuffled versions, whose keys match the shuffled order); chemistry rows of the keys were re-verified at high resolution, and computable answers were independently solved as cross-checks.
- Two flagged oddities, recorded in the bank's notes: 2012 Q61 prints an unbalanced equation in the original paper (kept verbatim); 2022 Q41's official answer (A) is chemically debatable (CO₂ arguably also qualifies).
- **Rights note:** these are official past-exam questions — fine as private teaching/practice material and as the design oracle, but a paid/shipped product must use original questions modeled on them, not verbatim copies.
