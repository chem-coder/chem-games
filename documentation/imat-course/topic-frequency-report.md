## Headline findings

1. **The Big Three still carry the exam — 36% — but acids–bases has taken the lead.** Chemical bonding (23), acids–bases (22), and stoichiometry (21) account for 66 of 184. Acids–bases is the top current-era topic: **10 of the last 60 questions** (2022–25), dominated by pH calculations — strong-acid/base pH, weak-acid √(Ka·C), neutral-salt recognition, Kw reasoning.

2. **Redox doubled its metronome in 2025.** After exactly one question every year for 14 years, 2025 asked **two** — both still pure oxidation-number work (assign +3 to Cl in HClO₂; "an oxidation number cannot be irrational"). Still zero cell/potential questions in 15 years. The Oxidation-State Trainer remains aimed at exactly what IMAT asks.

3. **2025's shock: stoichiometry was absent entirely** — the first zero in the topic's 15-year history apart from 2018. Solutions and matter also sat 2025 out; expect regression to the mean in 2026, not a trend.

4. **Organic-structure spiked to 3 in 2025, and two of the three are *biomolecule* questions** (glycogen as homopolysaccharide, amino acids' amine + carboxyl groups) sitting in the chemistry section — a flavor worth teaching deliberately: polysaccharide classes and amino-acid basics are now chemistry-section material.

5. **Gas laws confirmed their comeback** (a decade of near-zero, then 2023: 1, 2024: 3, 2025: 1 — the ideal-gas equation itself, as a straight recall item). **Kinetics woke up** after seven years (activation-energy definition). Equilibrium keeps its steady ~1 every other year; 2025's was a Le Châtelier with the classic solids-don't-shift trap.

6. **Near-zero topics remain:** thermochemistry (0 tagged in 15 years), galvanic/electrolytic cells (0), biochemistry-as-calculation (0 — it appears only as the descriptive organic questions above).

## Question-style notes

- Five options A–E, single best answer, no calculator; every computation resolves to clean mental arithmetic (dilution ratios, integer mole ratios, pH of 10⁻ⁿ solutions, √(10⁻⁸) = 10⁻⁴).
- Recurring stoichiometry patterns: Avogadro-scale atom counting (3×), percentage yield (3×), formula from % composition, limiting reagent.
- Figure-dependent questions are rare (~1–2/year recently, mostly organic structures); nearly everything works as text. 2025's chemistry section had none.

## Data caveats

- 2021–2025 questions and keys were transcribed from image-based PDFs (AcadIMAT shuffled versions, whose keys match the shuffled order); chemistry rows of the keys were re-verified at high resolution, and computable answers were independently solved as cross-checks. **2025's fifteen answers were solved blind first, then checked against the official key when it arrived the same day: 15/15 agreement.**
- Flagged oddities, recorded in the bank's notes: 2012 Q61 prints an unbalanced equation in the original paper (kept verbatim); 2022 Q41's official answer (A) is chemically debatable (CO₂ arguably also qualifies).
- **Rights note:** these are official past-exam questions — fine as private teaching/practice material and as the design oracle (the IMAT Decks drill at `imat-decks/` exists for Malcolm's prep on this basis), but a paid/shipped product must use original questions modeled on them, not verbatim copies.
