Molecule Builder

Drag-and-drop molecule building with self-balancing hydrogens. A sandbox for the future name → structure direction — nothing is graded here yet.

  1. Drag a carbon out of the tray — it arrives saturated, four hydrogens riding its rim.
  2. Bring two carbons close and a bond snaps in; each carbon sheds a hydrogen to pay for it.
  3. Click a bond to cycle single → double → triple → gone. Hydrogens re-balance instantly.
  4. Drop an atom back on the tray to remove it.

Prototype — mechanics only. The real rung will prompt a name (“build propene”), hide the formula readout, and grade the built structure on Check.
